GEX by strike
Green = calls (dealers long gamma, they dampen moves). Red = puts (dealers short gamma, they amplify moves). Bars are $ of hedging per 1% move. The flip is where total gamma crosses zero if price moved there. Computed in your browser from Deribit open interest and mark IV with Black-Scholes, assuming dealers are long calls and short puts (the usual GEX convention). Deribit only, so CME and OKX options aren't included. Refreshes every 5 min. Turn on Layers → GEX levels to see these lines on the chart.
