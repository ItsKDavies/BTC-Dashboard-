Regime by timeframe
How regimes are read
Trend strength is ADX(14): 25+ trending, under 20 ranging. Efficiency is net move ÷ total path over 20 bars (1 = straight line). Squeeze = Bollinger width in the bottom 15% of the last 150 bars with ADX under 23. Expansion = width and ATR both in the top quarter. Regime colours the playbook: trends reward pullback entries, ranges reward fading edges, squeezes reward waiting for the break.
Market structure
Swings are 3-bar pivots (2 on 1D). BOS = a close beyond the last swing in the trend's direction (continuation). CHoCH = the first close beyond the protected swing against it (the trend changes character). Next BOS is where the trend continues; CHoCH at is where it flips. ×N = breaks in a row.
Structure map
Dashed lines: BOS (green/red) and CHoCH (amber) at the swing that broke. Shaded boxes: open fair value gaps. Dotted outlines: order blocks still intact. Purple dotted: equal highs/lows where stops collect.

GEX by strike
Green = calls (dealers long gamma, they dampen moves). Red = puts (dealers short gamma, they amplify moves). Bars are $ of hedging per 1% move. The flip is where total gamma crosses zero if price moved there. Computed in your browser from Deribit open interest and mark IV with Black-Scholes, assuming dealers are long calls and short puts (the usual GEX convention). Deribit only, so CME and OKX options aren't included. Refreshes every 5 min. Turn on Layers → GEX levels to see these lines on the chart.
